Privacy Policy for Raqi Healthcare

Effective Date: October 8, 2024

Welcome to Raqi Healthcare ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

When you use Raqi Healthcare, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and profile picture.
  • Medical & Appointment Data: We collect information regarding the appointments you book, including the doctor's name, booking time, and any health notes or information you voluntarily provide during the booking process.
  • Payment Information: If you make a booking, your payment information (such as credit card details) is collected directly by our secure third-party payment processor, Stripe. We do not store your full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Device & Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about your device, such as your IP address, device type, operating system, and how you interact with the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To facilitate the booking of healthcare appointments between patients and doctors.
  • To process payments securely.
  • To send you push notifications, such as appointment reminders.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To improve our App’s functionality and user experience.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share your information in the following situations:

  • With Healthcare Providers: If you are a patient, your booking details and provided medical notes will be shared with the specific doctor you choose to book with.
  • With Service Providers: We share necessary data with trusted third-party services that help us operate, such as Firebase (Google) for database hosting and push notifications, and Stripe for payment processing.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time. To request account deletion, please contact us using the information provided below.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, despite our safeguards, no internet-based platform can be 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
